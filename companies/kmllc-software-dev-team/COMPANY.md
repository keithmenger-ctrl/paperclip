The KMLLC Software Dev Team is the engineering department of KMLLC. It is built from the "Build spec: Software Dev Team (Oct 21, 2026)" update and the follow-up change that sets CoacHub aside, on the Paperclip AI Operating Team project in Linear.

**Structure.** The Head of Software is the only agent Keith talks to. Every builder reports to the Head of Software, and each builder owns one active Linear project.

**Workflow.** Builders pull work from their Linear project, build it, and open a PR or branch. The Head of Software reviews every hand-off for quality and against the project goals, then sends it back or approves it. Approved work goes to Keith as one consolidated summary. Nothing deploys without Keith's thumbs-up. The team runs around the clock on scheduled routines.

**No builder:** CoacHub (set aside for now). Archived or scrapped: Coach App Mold, Kin, Menger Finance, Fractional Fitness Ops site, MPS Brand OS, Jarvis, /am Debrief.
